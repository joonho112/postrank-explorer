KRW’s archived reward path

5.5 · after Kline, Rose and Walters (2024), Figures 5A, 6 and 7

Disclosure and expected errors of archived cards across the λ grid.

KRW’s reward parameter λ changes the relative value of correct and incorrect directions. The archived grid contains a card at each hundredth from zero to one. Larger rewards generally correspond to greater disclosure, although individual archived changes need not be monotone. Each fixed card is evaluated using the released pair probabilities. The displays record grade counts, stated pairs, error rates and changes relative to the preceding grid row. The archived selection probabilities and solver tolerances remain relevant when interpreting deviations from properties of an exact optimal path under a single law.

Numerical illustrations

  • λ .25: published race card. The baseline card states 1,324 pairs, with E/N .136. Grade counts use a logarithmic scale; DR divides expected errors by every possible pair, separately from the disclosure share.
  • λ .08: first race split. Genuine Parts stands alone above the other 96 firms. Its standard error is smaller than AutoNation’s, despite AutoNation’s larger estimate. Risk .07406 is just below its own all-withheld reference; the comparison needs the extra decimal.
  • λ .01: one grade. The first positive λ states no direction. E/N is undefined. The complete table also retains the archived λ-zero endpoint, where postrank risk is unavailable rather than assigned a value.
  • λ .30: statements and silence. Against the preceding grid row, 830 pairs become stated and 92 become withheld. This comparison always uses neighbouring grid rows, irrespective of your previous slider position.
  • λ .98: disclosure dip. Between λ .97 and .98, disclosure falls from 4,620 to 4,614. More reward does not justify a claim that every archived step discloses more.
  • λ 1: end of grid. DR and E/N coincide at .268 because every pair is stated. The complete Purples matrix uses a different scale: it shades stated errors, unlike the Blues direction probabilities in the heat-map app.

Grid cards were computed with KRW’s code under archived probabilities and solver tolerances. Their evaluations here use the released postrank probabilities and supply no new optimality certificate. Race’s baseline card is Figure 7; gender is Figure 14 (§VIII). Figures 5A and 6 are in §V.E, and Figure 10A is in §VI.B.
