School-report changes along the withholding-cost path

5.11 · the public twelve-school CDE example

Exact optimal disclosure and changes in individual school-pair relations as τ varies.

With the posterior and report family fixed, minimum-risk school reports change at specific withholding costs. The path records total disclosure and every newly stated, newly withheld or reversed comparison at each change. Exact optimal disclosure is nondecreasing in τ under these conditions, but individual relations need not be retained. The display distinguishes the exact browser path from archived solver results, preserving the status and gap of each archived solve. Boundary tables include equal-risk reports and transitions below the slider’s displayed minimum.

Numerical illustrations

  • The usual setting uses τ .20. The optimum states 45 pairs in groups of sizes 3, 6, 3.
  • Past the last interior boundary uses τ .45. The optimum states 47 pairs in groups of sizes 4, 5, 3; the complete pair-change list gives the altered relations.
  • The early boundaries keeps the slider at its low end. The exact boundaries near .000162 and .000263 lie below that minimum but remain in the tables, including the step to 27 pairs.
  • The upper endpoint reaches τ .50. The path still states 47 pairs, and the chart retains all 13 archived solves.

The posterior, criterion weight and at-most-three-grade family stay fixed along this path. Exact optimal disclosure is nondecreasing in τ under those conditions. Individual relationships can change in more than one way, which is why the tables retain newly withheld and reversed pairs as well as new statements.

Changing τ changes the loss. A risk printed at one step is evaluated at that step’s own τ; cross-setting risk differences therefore concern different losses. At a boundary the app also lists all returned equal-risk reports and the comparison tolerance. The archived dots use the same family but were obtained with HiGHS, so their solver-status and gap fields remain separate from exact enumeration.

Data: California Department of Education, 2026 California School Dashboard chronic absenteeism data, academic year 2025–26, as adapted in postrank 1.1.0 (pr_case_cde). This deliberately spread twelve-school teaching subset is conditional on a statewide prior fitted to 5,834 eligible schools; prior-estimation uncertainty is not propagated. CDE source conditions apply; the data are not relicensed, are provided as is without warranty, and imply no CDE endorsement.

The analysis concerns directional reporting of chronic absence within this subset. It neither computes Dashboard status-and-change colours nor establishes school quality, causal effects, statewide optimality or calibration. Shared grades do not imply equal absence probabilities.
